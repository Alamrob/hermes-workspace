## Denied to every model agent

- Raw secret retrieval or credential display.
- Contract signature, terms acceptance, payment, purchasing or banking action.
- Administrative permission changes and destructive database/file operations.
- CAPTCHA/access-control/rate-limit bypass, fake accounts or identity impersonation.
- Unapproved prices, discounts, scope, dates, promises or legal positions.

## Hostinger deployment boundary

On the Hostinger VPS, only the HTTPS reverse proxy is public. Workspace, gateway, dashboard, PostgreSQL/Supabase adapter, Approval Gateway and connector broker stay on a private Docker network. Ports 8642 and 9119 are not exposed to the Internet. `TRUST_PROXY=1` is used only behind the configured sanitizing reverse proxy; cookies are secure; firewall and provider ACLs deny direct access.
