# Commercial Definitions

## Authority

- **Work order:** authenticated Codex instruction that fixes objective, scope, versions, autonomy, tools, channels, budget, volume, evidence and stop conditions.
- **Assignment:** bounded subset of a work order for one specialist.
- **Approval grant:** one-time authorization for one exact A3 action or bounded homogeneous batch.
- **A4 action:** action that an agent cannot perform even with a work order.

## Evidence

- **Fact:** directly supported statement with source, locator, capture time, confidence and verification method.
- **Inference:** interpretation based on identified fact IDs and labeled uncertainty.
- **Assumption:** unverified condition required to proceed; it must be visible and cannot authorize an external action.
- **Verified contact:** identity resolved to a person/role/account with provenance, freshness and confidence; contactability and permission are separate fields.
- **Signal:** time-bounded observed event that may affect fit or timing. A signal is not buying intent unless corroborated.

## Commercial records

- **Account:** organization or consumer household/entity represented in the authoritative CRM.
- **Contact:** identified person associated with an account; not automatically a lead.
- **Lead:** account/contact or inbound inquiry with known provenance and a declared qualification state.
- **Opportunity:** validated commercial process with defined offer, problem, owner, stage evidence and next action.
- **Customer:** closed-won party whose contract/billing source confirms entitlement.

## Quality and outcomes

- **Positive response:** response expressing relevant interest or willingness to continue; auto-replies and neutral acknowledgments do not count.
- **Qualified meeting:** meeting satisfying the approved qualification threshold and evidence requirements.
- **Progress:** verified advancement in stage evidence or customer outcome, not activity volume.
- **Duplicate:** record/action matching an authoritative deduplication key or unresolved identity cluster.
- **Suppressed:** target that must not be contacted through the relevant channel, regardless of commercial value.

## Versioning

Project, offer, ICP, policy, price catalog, message and proposal versions are immutable identifiers. An agent must reload authoritative data when its assignment version differs from current truth or the maximum freshness window is exceeded.
